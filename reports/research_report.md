# Agentic AI Research Report

## Research Question
What is Agentic AI and what are its challenges?

## Research Plan
1. Understand the research objective.
2. Identify relevant keywords.
3. Search the knowledge base.
4. Retrieve relevant information.
5. Analyze the findings.
6. Generate the report.

## Research Findings

### 1. Agentic AI Fundamentals

**Topic:** agentic ai

**Findings:** Agentic AI refers to AI systems that can pursue goals by planning tasks, making decisions, using tools, and adapting their actions based on results. AI agents can operate with varying levels of autonomy and human supervision.

**Source label:** IBM - Artificial Intelligence


### 2. AI Agents in Healthcare

**Topic:** healthcare

**Findings:** AI agents can assist healthcare workflows by organizing information, supporting administrative tasks, and helping retrieve relevant medical information. Clinical decisions require qualified healthcare professional oversight.

**Source label:** Healthcare AI Research Overview


### 3. AI Agents in Education

**Topic:** education

**Findings:** AI agents can support education through personalized learning assistance, tutoring, study planning, feedback generation, and administrative support. Human educators should supervise important educational decisions.

**Source label:** Educational AI Research Overview


### 4. AI Agent Challenges

**Topic:** challenges

**Findings:** AI agents can produce incorrect outputs, misuse tools, or misunderstand instructions. Important challenges include privacy, security, reliability, evaluation, and the need for human oversight.

**Source label:** AI Safety Research Overview


### 5. Multi-Agent Systems

**Topic:** multi agent systems

**Findings:** Multi-agent systems consist of multiple specialized agents that communicate or coordinate to solve complex problems. Different agents may handle planning, searching, analysis, and report generation.

**Source label:** Multi-Agent Systems Overview
